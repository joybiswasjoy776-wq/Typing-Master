Typing Tips

  • Keep your fingers on the home row.
  • Use both hands while typing.
  • Look at the screen instead of the keyboard.
  • Start slowly and focus on accuracy.
  • Practice for 15–20 minutes every day.
  • Try to keep your hands relaxed.
